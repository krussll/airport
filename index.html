Universal EU Mandate Alert: 100ml Liquid Limit Re-Imposed

As of September 1, 2024, the European Commission temporarily reinstated the mandatory 100ml single-container limit across all airports in the European Union, including those equipped with state-of-the-art C3 3D CT scanners.

Even if your departure airport allows liquids to remain inside your bag during screening, no individual container exceeding 100ml is permitted through security checkpoints.
