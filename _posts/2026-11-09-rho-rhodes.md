## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the screening rules at Rhodes Diagoras Airport?
Rhodes Airport uses conventional 2D X-ray scanners. Liquids must be in containers of 100ml or less inside a single 1L resealable clear bag, and laptops and tablets must be placed into separate trays.

### Can I take local Rhodian honey, olive oil, or Souma brandy through security?
Local jars of Rhodian pine/thyme honey, olive oil tins, and Souma pomace brandy over 100ml are strictly prohibited at checkpoint security. Pack them in your hold luggage or purchase duty-free items after clearing security.

### When are security lines busiest at Rhodes Airport?
Peak congestion occurs on **Wednesdays and Saturdays (10:00 - 16:30)**, driven by massive UK, Scandinavian, and German holiday charter rotations. Arrive 2.5 hours before departure.
