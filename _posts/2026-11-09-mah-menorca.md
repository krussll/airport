## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are 3D CT scanners available at Menorca Airport?
**No.** Menorca Airport operates conventional 2D X-ray systems across all passenger security lanes. Travelers must take out all liquids (containers $\le$ 100ml packed in a 1L clear plastic bag) and place laptops and tablets in dedicated plastic trays.

### Can I take Gin Xoriguer or Mahón cheese through Menorca security?
Bottles of Gin Xoriguer over 100ml purchased outside the airport are strictly banned from hand luggage and will be confiscated. Pack them in checked bags or buy them in duty-free after security. Semi-cured and cured Mahón cheese in solid blocks can pass through security, but soft cheeses or cheeses packaged in oil/brine are restricted under liquid regulations.

### Does Menorca Airport have FastTrack access?
Yes, Aena provides FastTrack lane access at MAH for eligible frequent flyers and business class passengers, as well as pay-per-use passes bookable via Aena.

### Do I have to remove smartwatches at Menorca checkpoint?
**No.** Standard smartwatches remain on your wrist. Only remove bulky jewelry, heavy metal wristbands, or coats/outerwear into the screening trays.
