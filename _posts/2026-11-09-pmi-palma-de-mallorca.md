## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Did Spain scrap the 100ml liquid rule or laptop removal at Palma?
**No.** Despite media reports of 3D CT scanner trials across Spanish airports, standard passenger lanes at Palma de Mallorca still require all liquids to be in containers of 100ml or less packed in a 1L clear plastic bag, and laptops and tablets must be placed into separate trays. Furthermore, European Commission regulations mandate a 100ml liquid limit across all EU airports regardless of scanner technology.

### Can I buy liquids over 100ml after passing PMI security?
**Yes.** Once you have cleared security screening on Level 4, all drinks, cosmetics, wines, and olive oils purchased in the airside retail shops or duty-free outlets are exempt from the 100ml rule. If you have an onward connecting flight at another EU hub, request a sealed STEB (Security Tamper Evident Bag) and keep the receipt inside.

### Does Palma Airport provide free plastic bags for liquids?
While plastic bag dispensers are sometimes available near the queue entrance, they frequently run empty during peak summer turnarounds. It is strongly advised to pack your liquids into a transparent resealable 1L bag before arriving at the airport.

### Are there FastTrack security lanes at PMI?
Yes. Aena operates a dedicated FastTrack security lane in the Central Security Hall for business class passengers, airline frequent flyer cardholders, and passengers who purchase Aena FastTrack access passes online.

### Do I need to take off my smartwatch or Apple Watch at security?
**No, standard smartwatches can remain on your wrist.** Unlike laptops and tablets, wearable electronics do not need to come out of your bag or be placed into plastic trays. You generally only need to remove your watch if it features a heavy or chunky metal band that sets off the walk-through metal detector, or if security staff request a secondary inspection. If you prefer to remove it proactively, zip it inside your carry-on bag rather than leaving it loose in an open tray.
