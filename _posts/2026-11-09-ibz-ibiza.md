## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Did Ibiza Airport scrap the 100ml liquid limit or laptop removal?
**No.** Ibiza Airport currently operates conventional 2D multi-view X-ray security lanes across its passenger departures concourse. All carry-on liquids must be in containers of 100ml or less and enclosed in one transparent resealable 1L plastic bag. Laptops, iPads, and large electronics must be removed into separate trays. In addition, the European Commission's mandate caps individual liquid containers at 100ml across all EU airports.

### Can I bring bottles of Hierbas Ibicencas liqueur through security?
**Only if purchased after security or checked in hold luggage.** Artisanal or supermarket bottles of Hierbas bought in Ibiza Town or San Antonio will be confiscated at security if they exceed 100ml. If you wish to take Hierbas home in your cabin baggage, purchase it from the airside duty-free stores after clearing the checkpoint and keep it in a sealed STEB tamper-evident bag with the receipt.

### Are smartwatches and wearable devices required to come off at Ibiza?
**No.** Standard smartwatches, Apple Watches, and fitness trackers can remain on your wrist when passing through security screening, unless requested for secondary manual inspection.

### When is security queue congestion worst at Ibiza Airport?
Peak congestion occurs on **Sunday afternoons and evenings (14:00 - 19:30)** and **Wednesday mornings**, corresponding with European club turnaround flights and weekend villa turnover waves. Arrive at least 2.5 hours prior during these windows.

### Is FastTrack security available at IBZ?
Yes. Aena provides a dedicated FastTrack security lane for qualifying airline status holders, business class travelers, and passengers who pre-purchase access online through the official Aena portal.
