## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I take Santorini Vinsanto or Assyrtiko wine in hand luggage?
**No.** Santorini wines purchased at local wineries or shops cannot pass security in carry-on luggage due to the 100ml limit. Pack wine bottles in checked luggage with bubble wrap or purchase them airside at the duty-free shop after screening.

### Does Santorini Airport have new 3D CT scanners?
**No.** Santorini Airport operates standard 2D X-ray screening machines. All passengers must extract their 100ml liquids bag and place laptops and tablets in separate screening trays.

### Can I bring volcanic pebbles or sand from Santorini beaches?
Taking volcanic pebbles, black sand, or red rocks from Santorini's famous Red Beach or Kamari is prohibited under local conservation regulations, and security officers regularly confiscate extracted mineral items from carry-on bags.

### Are smartwatches allowed during screening at Santorini Airport?
**Yes.** Standard smartwatches and Apple Watches may remain on your wrist during walk-through metal detector screening.
