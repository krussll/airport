## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which terminal does my flight depart from at Lanzarote Airport?
Lanzarote Airport has two terminals: **Terminal 1 (T1)** handles all international, UK, and mainland Spain flights; **Terminal 2 (T2)** handles regional inter-island flights between the Canary Islands (Binter, Canaryfly). Both operate standard 2D X-ray security.

### Do liquids and laptops stay in bags at Lanzarote?
**No.** Liquids must be under 100ml in a clear 1L resealable bag, and laptops and tablets must be placed into plastic trays in both T1 and T2.

### Can I bring Malvasía volcanic wine or Lanzarote volcanic sand in carry-on?
Full-sized bottles of Malvasía wine cannot pass security in carry-on baggage unless purchased duty-free post-security. Additionally, collecting and exporting volcanic sand or olivine crystals from Lanzarote beaches or Timanfaya is prohibited under conservation laws, and samples are confiscated at checkpoints.
