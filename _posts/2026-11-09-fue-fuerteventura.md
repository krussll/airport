## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the carry-on liquid and laptop rules at Fuerteventura Airport?
Fuerteventura Airport operates conventional 2D X-ray screening. All liquids must be in containers of 100ml or less in a clear 1-liter plastic bag, and laptops and tablets must be removed into trays.

### Can I bring Majorero goat cheese through security?
Solid, hard, and semi-cured Majorero cheese is permitted through security screening in hand luggage. However, soft creamy cheeses, goat cheese spreads, or cheeses packed in oil are classified as liquids/gels and will be confiscated if over 100ml.

### Can I take sand from Popcorn Beach (Playa del Bajo de la Burra)?
**Strictly prohibited.** Popcorn Beach's rhodolith fossils and white dune sands are legally protected. Airport security and environmental authorities screen for and confiscate these natural treasures, with hefty fines imposed on offenders.
