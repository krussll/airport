## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I bring Corfu Kumquat liqueur or Corfiot olive oil in carry-on?
**No, unless 100ml or under.** Bottles of Kumquat liqueur, Limoncello, and artisanal olive oil (such as The Governor) purchased on Corfu will be confiscated at security if they exceed 100ml. Check them in your hold luggage or buy them post-security in the Hellenic Duty Free shop with a sealed STEB bag and receipt.

### Does Corfu Airport have 3D CT scanners where liquids stay in bags?
**No.** Corfu Airport operates conventional 2D X-ray lanes. All liquids must be in containers $\le$ 100ml in a 1L clear plastic bag, and laptops and tablets must be removed into separate trays.

### Can I bring Greek Feta cheese in brine through CFU security?
Feta packaged in liquid brine is classified as a liquid and is confiscated if over 100ml. Solid vacuum-sealed dry cheeses are permitted.

### When does Corfu Airport experience peak security queues?
Peak congestion occurs on **Mondays and Fridays between 10:00 and 16:00** due to concentrated UK, German, and Italian charter flight banks. Arrive at least 2.5 hours prior to your flight.
