## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I bring Cretan olive oil or thyme honey in hand luggage through Heraklion Airport?
**No.** Cretan olive oil and honey are classified as liquids and will be immediately confiscated if container sizes exceed 100ml, even if sealed in original farm cans or gift packs. Always pack olive oil and honey in checked hold luggage (well-padded) or purchase post-security in the Hellenic Duty Free shops.

### Are there 3D CT scanners at Heraklion Airport?
**No.** Heraklion Airport (Nikos Kazantzakis) relies on legacy 2D X-ray equipment. You must unpack your 100ml liquids bag and place laptops and tablets in trays for screening.

### Can I bring Feta or Graviera cheese in carry-on?
Hard cured cheeses like Graviera are generally allowed in cabin baggage. However, fresh Feta or cheeses preserved in brine or olive oil are treated as liquids and will be seized if exceeding 100ml.

### How early should I arrive at Heraklion Airport in summer?
Heraklion Airport experiences severe terminal congestion on **Tuesdays and Saturdays (09:00 - 15:30)** due to overlapping European charter flights. Allow at least 2.5 to 3 hours prior to departure during high season.
