## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does Chania Airport allow laptops and liquids to remain in bags?
**No.** Chania Airport operates conventional 2D X-ray security lanes managed by Fraport Greece. Passengers must remove their transparent 1L liquids bag (max 100ml containers) and place laptops and tablets in security trays.

### Will security at Chania confiscate Cretan raki (tsikoudia) or olive oil?
**Yes.** Any bottles of raki, tsikoudia, or artisanal olive oil bought in Chania or local villages exceeding 100ml will be confiscated. To transport them safely, pack them wrapped inside checked baggage or buy sealed duty-free bottles after the security gates.

### Do smartwatches need to be removed at Chania security?
**No.** Standard smartwatches can stay on your wrist unless the walk-through metal detector triggers an alarm or security staff instruct secondary screening.
