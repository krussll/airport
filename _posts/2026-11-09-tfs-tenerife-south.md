## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does Tenerife South (TFS) require liquids and laptops out?
**Yes.** Standard screening lanes at Tenerife South require all liquids to be in containers of 100ml or less inside a single clear 1-liter plastic bag, and all laptops, tablets, and gaming consoles must be placed in separate security trays.

### Can I bring Canarian Honey Rum (Ron Miel) or Mojo sauces in hand luggage?
**No, unless 100ml or under.** Jars of Mojo Picón and bottles of Ron Miel exceeding 100ml are among the most frequently seized items at TFS security. Always pack them into checked hold luggage or buy them airside post-security in sealed duty-free bags.

### Can I take volcanic sand or Teide rocks home in my carry-on?
**No.** Removing volcanic sand, pebbles, or rocks from Teide National Park or protected Canary beaches is illegal under Spanish environmental conservation laws. Airport security and Guardia Civil actively confiscate extracted geological samples at screening checkpoints and refer violators for environmental fines.

### When are security queues heaviest at Tenerife South?
TFS experiences massive passenger surge windows on **Tuesdays and Fridays between 11:30 and 16:30**, driven by dense UK and German charter turnaround flights. Arriving 2.5 to 3 hours early is strongly recommended on these days.
